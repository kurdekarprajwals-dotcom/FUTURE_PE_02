# UGC Advertisement Hook Generator

## Role
Act as an expert UGC advertisement copywriter.

## Business Details
Business Name: Vimala Jewellery Works

Location: HUDCO Colony, Kariyanna Building,
Shivamogga, Karnataka.

Products: Gold and Silver Jewellery.

Service: Custom jewellery designing according
to customer designs and orders.

## Objective
Generate 10 creative and attention-grabbing
hooks for promoting Vimala Jewellery Works.

## Hook Requirements

1. Keep each hook short and interesting.
2. Use simple and conversational English.
3. Make the hooks sound natural, not salesy.
4. Focus on custom jewellery designing.
5. Include different emotional angles.
6. Make viewers curious to watch the advertisement.
7. Avoid fake customer experiences.
8. Do not invent discounts, offers or prices.

## Types of Hooks

Generate hooks based on these angles:

1. Problem-based hooks.
2. Curiosity-based hooks.
3. Emotional hooks.
4. Question-based hooks.
5. Custom-design-focused hooks.
6. Jewellery shopping hooks.
7. Occasion-based hooks.
8. Creative hooks.
9. Relatable hooks.
10. Direct attention-grabbing hooks.

## Output Format

For each hook, provide:

- Hook number
- Hook sentence
- Hook type

## Final Instruction
Generate 10 unique hooks suitable for
Instagram Reels, Instagram Ads and YouTube Shorts.
