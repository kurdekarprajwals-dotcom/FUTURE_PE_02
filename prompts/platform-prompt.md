# Platform-Specific UGC Advertisement Generator

## Role
Act as a social media marketing expert and
UGC advertisement scriptwriter.

## Business Details
Business Name: Vimala Jewellery Works

Location: HUDCO Colony, Kariyanna Building,
Shivamogga, Karnataka.

Products: Gold and Silver Jewellery.

Service: Custom jewellery designing according
to customer designs and orders.

## Objective
Create platform-specific UGC advertisement
scripts to promote the jewellery business.

## Platform 1: Instagram Reels
- Create a 20-30 second video script.
- Start with an attention-grabbing hook.
- Use natural and conversational language.
- Include visual suggestions for each scene.
- End with a clear call to action.

## Platform 2: Instagram Ads
- Create a 15-30 second advertisement.
- Focus on attracting potential customers.
- Highlight custom jewellery designing.
- Include a strong opening hook.
- Add a clear conversion-focused CTA.

## Platform 3: YouTube Shorts
- Create a 20-30 second video script.
- Use an interesting opening.
- Keep the content engaging.
- Include simple scene descriptions.
- End with a memorable CTA.

## Content Requirements
1. Use simple English.
2. Maintain an authentic UGC tone.
3. Avoid overly promotional language.
4. Focus on customer needs and interests.
5. Highlight custom gold and silver jewellery.
6. Do not invent prices, discounts or offers.
7. Do not create fake customer testimonials.

## Output Format
For each platform, provide:

1. Advertisement title.
2. Opening hook.
3. Complete video script.
4. Scene-by-scene visual suggestions.
5. Call to action.
6. Suggested caption.
7. Relevant hashtags.

## Final Instruction
Generate unique advertisements for all three
platforms while maintaining the same business
identity and message.
