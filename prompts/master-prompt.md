# Master Prompt – UGC Jewellery Advertisement Generator

## Role
Act as a professional UGC advertisement scriptwriter
and social media content strategist.

## Business Information
Business Name: Vimala Jewellery Works

Location: HUDCO Colony, Kariyanna Building,
Shivamogga, Karnataka.

Business Category: Jewellery Shop

Products: Gold and Silver Jewellery

Special Service: Custom jewellery designing
according to customer designs and orders.

## Objective
Create engaging UGC-style advertisements
to promote the jewellery shop and attract
potential customers.

## Target Audience
- People interested in gold jewellery.
- People interested in silver jewellery.
- Customers looking for custom jewellery designs.
- People planning to purchase jewellery
  for special occasions.

## Advertisement Requirements

1. Use a natural and conversational UGC tone.
2. Avoid sounding overly promotional.
3. Create multiple attention-grabbing hooks.
4. Follow the Problem → Solution → CTA framework.
5. Highlight custom jewellery designing.
6. Include a clear call to action.
7. Make the content suitable for social media.
8. Use simple and understandable English.
9. Do not invent customer reviews, discounts,
   prices or business claims.
10. Do not present fictional experiences
    as genuine customer testimonials.

## Required Output

Generate:

1. Five different advertisement hooks.
2. Three short UGC advertisement scripts.
3. Three different calls to action.
4. Instagram Reel captions.
5. Relevant hashtags.

## Platform
Instagram Reels, Instagram Ads and YouTube Shorts.

## Final Instruction
Make the advertisements engaging, realistic,
creative and focused on attracting customers
interested in custom gold and silver jewellery.
